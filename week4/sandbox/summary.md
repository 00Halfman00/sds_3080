# Summaries of Top News Stories

## BBC News (World)
*Iran warns it will retaliate if US attacks, as hundreds killed in protests*: Iran’s leadership has issued a threat of retaliation against any US military action amid ongoing protests that have seen numerous casualties. The BBC reports that demonstrators describe the situation as **"a war zone,"** with streets **"full of blood.**

## The Guardian (World)
*Attempt to overturn the Gambia’s ban on FGM heard by supreme court*: Religious leaders and an MP in the Gambia have petitioned the country’s supreme court to strike down the ban on female genital mutilation. The case follows a 2024 incident in which two babies bled to death after undergoing FGM.
